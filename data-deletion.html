Data Deletion

Google Play account-deletion / data-deletion disclosure for Qibla Direction

Qibla Direction does not create user accounts and does not store a personal cloud profile on our servers. Data lives on your device.

Delete data on your device

  1. Open Android Settings
  2. Go to Apps → Qibla Direction
  3. Tap Storage
  4. Tap Clear storage / Clear data

This removes local preferences, language choice, onboarding state, prayer overrides, notification toggles, and dhikr history stored by the App.

Uninstall

Uninstalling Qibla Direction also removes the App’s local data from that device (unless your OEM backs up app data separately).

Revoke permissions

  • Location: Settings → Apps → Qibla Direction → Permissions
  • Notifications: Settings → Apps → Qibla Direction → Notifications

Support emails

If you contacted us by email, you may request deletion of that correspondence by writing to waseemdeveloper521@gmail.com with subject “Delete my support email”.

Account deletion

Because the App has no in-app accounts, there is no separate “delete account” flow inside the App. Clearing data or uninstalling is the complete deletion path for App-stored information.
