Age & Audience

For Google Play content rating, target audience, and Families policy

Qibla Direction is not designed for children, is not directed to children under 13, and is not intended for inclusion in the Google Play Families program.

Target audience

  • Primary users: Adults and teens who pray and want Qibla / prayer tools
  • Minimum age: 13+
  • Not for: Preschool, children, or “designed for families / kids” experiences

Play Console guidance (how we declare it)

  • Target age group: 13–15, 16–17, and 18+ as applicable — do not select under-13 groups as primary targets if the App is not for children.
  • Store presence: Listing, screenshots, and description should not market the App as a children’s product.
  • Families policy: Do not enroll this App in Designed for Families / Families categories.
  • Ads / appeal: The App does not include child-directed advertising experiences.

Why this App is not for children

  • It requests location for religious navigation and timing tools
  • It may schedule system notifications
  • Content and UX assume independent use by teens/adults, not supervised kids’ play
  • We do not provide a child-specific mode or parental dashboard

Parental note

If you are a parent or guardian, please do not allow children under 13 to use this App. Use Android parental controls / Family Link as appropriate for your household.
