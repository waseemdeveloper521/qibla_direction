Common help

Qibla seems inaccurate

  • Enable location permission for the App
  • Calibrate the compass (move the phone in a figure-8)
  • Keep away from magnets, metal cases, and car mounts
  • Refresh location from the Qibla screen

Prayer times look wrong

  • Confirm location permission and city label on Home
  • Check calculation method / madhab in Settings
  • You can tap a prayer row to set a custom time if needed

No Azan / notifications

  • Turn on Notifications in the App and in Android Settings
  • Disable battery optimizations that kill background alarms
  • Ensure each prayer’s alert toggle is enabled

Language or onboarding

  • Change language from Home quick actions or Settings
  • Clear App data to see onboarding again (this resets local preferences)
