Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026 · App: Qibla Direction (com.qibladirection.app)

This app is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for use by children. See our Age & Audience page.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Qibla Direction (“we”, “us”, “the App”) handles information when you use our mobile application on Android.

Contact: support@qibladirection.app (replace this address with your real support email before publishing if needed).

2. Summary

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • Location is used on-device to calculate Qibla direction and prayer times.
  • Preferences, language, and dhikr history are stored on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The App is not designed for children.

3. Information we process

3.1 Location (approximate / precise)

With your permission, the App accesses your device location to:

  • Calculate the Qibla bearing from your position toward the Kaaba
  • Compute local prayer times
  • Show a place name (city / country) via reverse geocoding

Location is processed on your device for these features. We do not operate a user-profile backend that stores a history of your movements.

3.2 App settings & local data

Stored on your device (for example via local storage), including:

  • Language preference and onboarding status
  • Prayer calculation settings and optional time overrides
  • Notification toggles for prayer alerts
  • Tasbih / dhikr counts and local history

3.3 Notifications

If you enable notifications, the App schedules local prayer / Azan alerts on your device. Notification permission is optional and can be revoked in system settings.

3.4 Device & diagnostics

Standard platform services (Google Play / Android) may collect crash, performance, or install analytics according to Google’s policies. We do not independently run a separate ad-tracking SDK in the App for interest-based advertising.

4. How we use information

  • Provide Qibla, prayer times, calendar, and tasbih features
  • Remember your preferences
  • Schedule prayer alerts you choose to enable
  • Improve reliability and respond to support requests you send us

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by platform providers (e.g., Google Play services, device OS location APIs) solely to operate the App’s features. If you email support, we will use your message and address only to respond.

6. Data retention

Local App data remains on your device until you clear App storage, uninstall the App, or reset settings inside the App (where available). Support emails are retained only as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Your choices & rights

  • Deny or revoke location permission in system settings
  • Deny or revoke notification permission
  • Clear App data or uninstall to remove on-device data
  • See Data Deletion for steps

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us to make a request.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for children’s use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us via support email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We rely on on-device storage and platform security. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure; please keep your device updated and locked.

10. International users

The App may be used worldwide. By using the App you understand that device and platform processing may occur according to your device region and Google’s infrastructure.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Effective date” above will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised Policy.
